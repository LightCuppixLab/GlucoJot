## 2. What GlucoJot Does

GlucoJot is a personal logbook for manually recording and viewing blood glucose values. Depending on the version and your access level, features may include:

- manual readings with date, time, context, and optional notes;
- target-range summaries;
- averages and charts over selected periods;
- an indicative eA1c estimate;
- daily logbook and filters;
- JSON backup; and
- PDF or CSV export.

The App does not measure glucose, does not connect to a continuous glucose monitor, and does not read from or write to Apple Health.
