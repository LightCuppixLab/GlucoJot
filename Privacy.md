# Privacy Policy for GlucoJot

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GlucoJot (the “App”) handles information. GlucoJot is designed as a privacy-first, local-first personal logbook for manually entered blood glucose readings.

## 1. Who We Are

GlucoJot is developed by Light Cup Pix Lab, Padova, Italy (“Light Cup Pix Lab”, “we”, “us”, or “our”).

Privacy contact: lightcuppixlab.dev@gmail.com

For the limited personal data that we may receive directly, such as a support email, Light Cup Pix Lab is the data controller.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to the GlucoJot iPhone app. The App does not provide an account, social profile, advertising service, or developer-operated cloud storage for your glucose log.

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

### 3.1 Glucose log

The App lets you manually enter:

- blood glucose values;
- date and time;
- context, such as fasting, before a meal, after a meal, evening, night, or exercise; and
- an optional free-text note.

The App also calculates summaries from those entries, such as averages, values in the target range, trends, counts, and an indicative estimated A1c (“eA1c”).

These entries and calculated values are stored locally in the App’s private storage on your iPhone. Light Cup Pix Lab does not receive or have access to your glucose readings or notes.

GlucoJot does not connect to continuous glucose monitors, does not read from or write to Apple Health, and does not measure glucose automatically.

### 3.2 Preferences and local technical data

The App stores settings such as:

- preferred unit (mg/dL or mmol/L);
- target range;
- selected app language;
- onboarding status; and
- a local cache of Pro entitlement status.

These settings are stored on your device using Apple system storage. They are used only to provide and remember App functionality.

### 3.3 Files created by you

You may create JSON backups and, where available, PDF or CSV exports. These files are generated locally. When you use the iOS share sheet, you choose the destination and recipient. Once shared, the selected destination or third-party app handles the file under its own privacy terms.

Temporary export files may remain in the App’s temporary storage until iOS removes them or the App is deleted.

## 4. Information We Do Not Collect

GlucoJot does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, or developer-operated crash reporting.

We do not collect your:

- glucose readings or notes;
- name, date of birth, or contact list;
- location;
- photos, camera content, or microphone recordings;
- Apple advertising identifier;
- browsing history; or
- HealthKit data.

Because the App does not track you across apps or websites, it does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

## 5. Purchases and Subscriptions

GlucoJot may offer optional monthly and yearly auto-renewable Pro subscriptions.

Purchases, billing, refunds, and subscription management are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card or other payment instrument details. The App checks your current entitlement through Apple to determine whether Pro features should be unlocked.

Apple may process purchase, account, device, and diagnostic information under Apple’s own privacy policy and App Store terms. Light Cup Pix Lab does not combine your App Store purchase information with your glucose log.

## 6. Support Communications

If you choose “Write to Support” or otherwise email us, we receive the information you voluntarily include, such as your email address, message, and any attachments.

We use support communications only to answer your request, troubleshoot the issue, protect against abuse, and keep records where reasonably necessary. Please do not send glucose readings, medical records, passwords, or other sensitive information by email.

Support email is processed by our email service provider and is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for support, security, legal, or record-keeping purposes. You may request deletion, subject to any legal obligation requiring retention.

## 7. Device Backups

Depending on your iPhone and Apple Account settings, Apple’s iCloud Backup or a computer backup may include the App and its local data. Those backups are controlled and processed by Apple or by you, not by Light Cup Pix Lab. Review your Apple backup settings if you do not want App data included.

## 8. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data and do not share your glucose log with advertisers or data brokers.

Third-party services may process limited information only when you choose or use the relevant function:

- **Apple:** App distribution, StoreKit purchases, subscription management, refunds, and optional device backups.
- **Your chosen share destination:** Files exported through the iOS share sheet.
- **Email provider:** Messages that you voluntarily send to support.
- **External websites:** Links opened from the App, including the Privacy Policy, Terms, subscription-management page, or support resources, are governed by the destination’s own privacy practices.

## 9. Retention and Deletion

Your glucose log and settings remain on your device until you edit or delete them.

You can:

- access and correct readings in the App;
- export a JSON backup and, where available, PDF or CSV files;
- delete individual readings;
- use **Settings → Your Data → Delete All Data** to remove the App’s glucose log; or
- delete the App to remove its local container from the device, subject to any independent device backup you control.

Deleting the App does not cancel an App Store subscription. Subscription management is handled separately in your Apple Account.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal data processed by a controller.

Because Light Cup Pix Lab cannot access the glucose log stored on your device, most choices are exercised directly in the App as described above. For personal data that we actually hold, such as a support email, you may contact us at lightcuppixlab.dev@gmail.com.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In Italy, the supervisory authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

## 11. Legal Bases for Limited Off-Device Processing

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, we process support communications as necessary to respond to your request and provide support, and on the basis of our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving support. We retain information where required to comply with legal obligations or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Apple independently determines the legal bases for information it processes through the App Store, StoreKit, and Apple services.

## 12. Security

Local App data benefits from the security and data-protection features of iOS and your device passcode settings. No storage method is completely risk-free. You are responsible for protecting access to your iPhone, keeping iOS updated, and securely handling exported files and backups.

## 13. International Processing

Apple, your chosen share destination, or our email provider may process information in countries other than your own under their respective terms and safeguards. Light Cup Pix Lab does not transfer your glucose log because we do not receive it.

## 14. Children

The App is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal data may contact us to request its deletion.

## 15. Not a Medical Device

GlucoJot is a personal record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and does not provide medical advice. Its target-range status, averages, trends, and eA1c are informational summaries of manually entered data and may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Do not use the App for emergency decisions or to start, stop, or change medication or treatment. Seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

## 16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the App or applicable requirements change. We will revise the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, provide notice in the App or on the policy page.
